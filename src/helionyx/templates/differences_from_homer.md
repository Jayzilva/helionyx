1. The battery always discharges before the genset starts; HOMER instead compares marginal costs. The exception is the load-following and cycle-charging rules, which apply when the battery cannot cover the load.
2. There is no operating reserve. Capacity shortage equals unserved energy.
3. The battery uses an idealised energy-reservoir model, not a kinetic battery model.
4. Only one genset is modelled, with no multi-generator priority.
5. The time step is hourly, and one year represents the whole project life.
6. Only AC coupling is supported.
